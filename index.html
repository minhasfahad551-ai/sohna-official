WELCOME TO SOHNA

Where Expert Grooming Meets Clinical Precision.

Crafting confidence since 1998. Founded by Mehmood Ahmad, SOHNA began as a classic salon and has evolved across nearly three decades of professional mastery. Expanding from master barbering into Clinical skin care, we established Sohna Aesthetics in 2023 and introduced our private Sohna Grooming Studio in 2026. Today, our legacy lives on across three distinct locations—our signature Men's Salon, an exclusive Unisex VIP Grooming Studio, and an advanced Unisex Aesthetic Clinic.
